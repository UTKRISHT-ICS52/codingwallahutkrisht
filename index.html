About Me

I am a researcher and technology enthusiast passionate about leveraging Artificial Intelligence, Robotics and emerging technologies to solve real-world challenges. My work focuses on bridging the gap between research and practical implementation, transforming innovative ideas into impactful solutions. Currently, I am involved in research and development projects spanning Machine Learning, IoT, intelligent systems and sustainable technologies. Alongside my research journey, I actively contribute to startup initiatives, software development projects and innovation-driven programs that foster technological advancement and societal impact. As an author and researcher, I am particularly interested in exploring interdisciplinary applications of AI, data-driven decision making and next-generation intelligent systems. I believe that meaningful innovation emerges at the intersection of research, collaboration and continuous learning. Beyond academics, I enjoy building projects, participating in hackathons, contributing to technical communities and collaborating with individuals who are passionate about creating technology for a better future.
